ASIX AX88179B built into the round-2 USB-C dock, as UsbHostManager logged it on the
motorola edge 30 neo (host). VID:PID 0B95:1790, same pair as round 1's AX88179A
(0B95:1790) but a newer silicon revision (mProductName=AX88179B).

The device stays in configuration 1 (the vendor form) on every plug on this host. The
CDC configurations exist in the descriptor but are never made active, and — this is the
point of the R1 FAIL — UsbDevice.getInterfaceList() as seen by the app returns only
configuration 1's single interface, so the policy's CDC-sibling veto has nothing to act
on.
